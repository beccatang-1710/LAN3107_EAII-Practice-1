EAII Practice 1 — Meetings: Improving the Business of Home Essence

Presented by Rebecca Tang

Task Overview

You are on the marketing team of Home Essence, a retail store selling eco-friendly home essentials in Hong Kong. You and your colleagues are going to hold a meeting to discuss plans to improve the business. You should:

  • give your own suggestion(s) on the agenda items
  • explain the reason(s) for your suggestion(s)
  • listen actively and respond to others' suggestions by expressing preferences, agreement, or disagreement
  • reach a consensus on agenda items 2–4

As a group, you must cover all the agenda items below.

Meeting Details

Home Essence Marketing Team 1 October 20-- at 2:30 p.m. in the Meeting Room

Agenda

  1. Opening the meeting
  2. Monthly discounted products for November
  3. Promotion strategies for attracting customers

    Possible strategies:

    • Advertisements
    • Incentive-based offers (coupons/vouchers)
    • Product placement
    • Collaborations with influencers on social media
  4. AOB

    Ways to promote a greener culture in the store:

    • Set up a refillable water station
    • Use of e-receipts
  5. Closing the meeting
